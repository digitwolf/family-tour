# Trip Goal and Parameters

## Trip Goal

Design a scenic, safety-first **family motorcycle coast tour** in the Pacific Northwest whose purpose is to **build the family's touring skills and a love of touring**, anchored on a still-new rider. Eight days, round trip from home, leaving August 15, 2026.

The trip combines an easy, confidence-building ferry start with steadily richer scenery — the wild Olympic Peninsula and Ruby Beach, the whole northern Oregon Coast with two glamping camps, and a Portland city finale — while keeping every day gentle, breakable, and free of high-speed roads.

## The family

* **Galiya — Rider 2 (sets the pace).** Recently earned her motorcycle endorsement; rides her own **Kawasaki W230** (233 cc, happiest 55–65 mph, ~3.4-gal tank). A newer solo rider — her confidence governs the route and daily length; she rides up front. A big foodie; loves the coast, scenery and lighthouses.
* **Ruslan — Rider 1.** Seasoned (BMW R1300GS; a Morocco ADV trip). Rides the GS carrying their son. Not the limiting rider — serve him with riding quality.
* **Aslan — 6, passenger.** Rides pillion with dad. Loves toys/souvenirs, animals, hands-on museums, expedition-style tents and the cheese factory. Can do up to ~6h on a good day but not long days back-to-back.

## Rider & safety priorities

Built around Galiya's still-new-rider confidence and Aslan's comfort:

* No freeways all week — quiet backroads, coastal highway and gentle valley two-lanes; the one planned exception is the short Day-8 I-5 run home.
* Short days between the bases; the ferry and frequent stops break up Day 1; never two long days in a row — the one long transfer day (Day 2) follows the easy ferry opener, and Day 8 is a short freeway run.
* Stops every 60–90 minutes; nothing technical, wet-aggressive, or after dark.
* Full child-pillion setup on the GS (fitted helmet/armor, peg reach, top-box backrest, grab strap, intercom).
* Plan fuel around the W230's small tank (the Olympic west end is the thin stretch); pack for coast fog and warm valley afternoons.

## Route

Woodinville → **Edmonds–Kingston ferry** → Port Angeles → **Lake Crescent** → **Forks** (safari tent) → **Ruby Beach** → Lake Quinault → Long Beach → **Astoria** (Cannery Pier Hotel & Spa) → Cannon Beach → **Tillamook** (cheese, capes) → **Two Capes Lookout** (★ 2-night glamping base + Three Capes rest day) → Depoe Bay whales → Newport → **Yachats** (★ 2-night nice-hotel base + Cape Perpetua rest day) → **Alsea Hwy** → Corvallis → 99W wine country → **Portland** (nice hotel) → optional St. Johns Bridge photo → I-5 north → Castle Rock lunch → home. ≈ 945 mi over 8 days.

## Lodging & food

* Almost every night is its own event: safari tent → pier hotel → geodesic domes → oceanfront lodge → city hotel. Secure/easy motorcycle parking everywhere; always confirm parking + passenger/child rules (and the glamping guest fit) before booking; book the August coast nights far ahead.
* Food is part of the fun — the **Coast Food Trail**: Astoria fish-and-chips, Tillamook cheese & ice cream, Netarts Bay oysters, Yachats chowder, and a Portland food-cart finale.

## Trip style summary

A gentle, premium family coast tour — wild beaches, memorable camps, short days between two real rest bases, and a still-new rider stacking a second successful tour. Not a mileage challenge or an endurance ride.
