# Two Capes Lookout — Tierra Del Mar

> Design-forward glamping between Cape Lookout and Cape Kiwanda — two nights of geodesic domes, dairy-country ice cream and the Three Capes at walking pace.

- **Region:** Oregon Coast · Tillamook County
- **Itinerary:** Days 3–4 · 2 nights (arrive Mon Aug 17 · rest day Tue Aug 18)
- **Stop type:** stay
- **Ride to here:** 100 mi (Astoria via Cannon Beach, Tillamook & Cape Meares, Day 3)

## About
**Two Capes Lookout** is a 58-acre glamping resort at Tierra Del Mar, tucked between the forested headland of **Cape Lookout** to the north and the sandstone dune of **Cape Kiwanda** to the south — hence the name. Opened in 2025, it strings **geodesic domes and mirrored cabins** through old-growth forest, wetlands and a historic quarry with its own year-round waterfall; the beach is a short walk, Pacific City is five minutes down the road, and the whole **Three Capes Scenic Loop** is the neighborhood. This is the trip's first two-night base: bikes parked for a full day, a dome in the trees, and the coast at kid pace.

The ride in is a greatest-hits reel of the north Oregon coast: out of Astoria, under Haystack Rock at **Cannon Beach**, over Neahkahnie Mountain to Manzanita, around Tillamook Bay — and then the family's requested marquee stop, the **Tillamook Creamery**, before the little loop road out to **Cape Meares Lighthouse**, the Octopus Tree and the puffin colonies of **Three Arch Rocks** off Oceanside. The rest day belongs to the capes: the dory boats and giant climbable dune at Pacific City, fresh **Netarts Bay oysters**, the Cape Lookout trail through Sitka spruce — or nothing at all but the beach and the firepit.

## Things to see & do
- **The dome itself** — A geodesic dome (or mirror cabin) in the forest with real beds and heat; firepits, a quarry waterfall and trails on the property — for Aslan the lodging is the headline attraction (**glamping night 2 of the trip — the base**).
- **Tillamook Creamery (on the way in)** — The famous free self-guided **cheese-factory viewing gallery**, cheese tasting and the legendary **ice-cream counter** — the marquee foodie-and-kid stop of the whole coast run (**Galiya + Aslan double win**).
- **Cape Meares (lighthouse + the Octopus Tree)** — Oregon's shortest lighthouse on the cliffs and the giant candelabra-shaped Sitka spruce beside it, with seabird views over Three Arch Rocks (**short walks, big payoff**).
- **Three Arch Rocks / Oceanside** — Three huge sea rocks half a mile off Oceanside's beach — the oldest wildlife refuge in the West, home to puffins, murres and sea lions; binoculars from the sand (**wildlife for Aslan**).
- **Cape Kiwanda / Pacific City** — The sandstone headland with colorful **dory boats** launching straight off the beach, a towering **sand dune** kids climb and run down, and Pelican Brewing on the sand (**the rest day's main event**).
- **Cape Lookout trail** — The middle cape: a 2.5-mile cliff-top trail through Sitka spruce to the tip of the promontory, with views from Cape Kiwanda to Cape Meares — go as far as small legs last (**easy family hike**).
- **Netarts Bay** — The quiet oyster bay behind the spit: crabbing docks, herons, and the freshest oysters of the trip at the water's edge (**foodie + wildlife**).
- **Tillamook Air Museum (optional)** — Vintage aircraft in the vast WWII wooden blimp hangar south of Tillamook — a Ruslan-and-Aslan detour if the rest day wants a motor (**hangar scale alone is worth it**).

## What to eat
- **Tillamook Creamery** — Squeaky cheese curds, cheddar tastings, and waffle-cone scoops at the source — plus grilled cheese and mac-and-cheese for the easiest kid lunch on the coast.
- **The Schooner Restaurant & Lounge (Netarts)** — Netarts Bay oysters raw, grilled and fried, chowder and local catch with the bay at your feet — the grown-ups' dinner pick.
- **Pelican Brewing — Pacific City** — Beachfront brewpub at the foot of Cape Kiwanda: chowder, fish tacos, burgers and house beers with a Haystack Rock view (**family favorite**).
- **Meridian Restaurant & Bar (Headlands Lodge)** — Pacific City's upscale coastal kitchen for a nicer night out five minutes from the domes.
- **Stimulus Espresso Café (Pacific City)** — Proper espresso and pastries above the beach — the rest-day slow morning.
- **Doryland Pizza (Pacific City)** — Pizza in the old dory-fleet cannery building — Aslan's simplest win, steps from the dune.
