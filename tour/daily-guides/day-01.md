## Day 1 — Ferry to the Rainforest (Woodinville → Forks, WA)

The gentle opener: a short ride to Edmonds, the ferry across Puget Sound, then US-101 the length of the Olympic Peninsula's north side — Port Gamble's mill-town bluff, a Port Angeles chowder lunch, and ten shoreline miles along fjord-blue Lake Crescent — before the timber country delivers the family to Forks and the trip's first glamping night in a safari tent.

**Schedule:** coffee in Woodinville · mid-morning ferry · Port Gamble ~11:00 · Port Angeles lunch ~12:45 · Lake Crescent ~14:30 · Forks ~16:00 · Timber Museum & Twilight sign · safari-tent check-in · dinner ~18:00.

### Where to eat

**Lunch — Port Angeles**
- **Yodelin Broth Company** (4.7★) — the town's best bowl of chowder; warm broth bowls for everyone. [Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Yodelin+Broth+Company+Port+Angeles)
- **Dead Low Pizza Company** (4.9★) — slice-shop simplicity for Aslan. [Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Dead+Low+Pizza+Port+Angeles)
- **Downriggers on the Water** (4.3★) — harbor-view sit-down with a kids' menu. [Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Downriggers+on+the+Water+Port+Angeles)

**Dinner — Forks**
- **Blakeslees Bar and Grill** (4.1★) — the family grill; kids' menu and big portions. [Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Blakeslees+Bar+and+Grill+Forks+WA)
- **Sully's Drive-In** (4.3★) — the Twilight-famous Bella Burger and milkshakes. [Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Sully's+Drive-In+Forks+WA)
- **Marble & Oak** (4.3★) — Forks' nicest table for a grown-up dinner. [Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Marble+and+Oak+Forks+WA)

### Things to do
- **~10:00 — Edmonds–Kingston ferry:** bikes stage and load first; ~30 minutes across the Sound. [Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Edmonds+Ferry+Terminal)
- **~11:00 — Port Gamble:** preserved 1850s mill town, coffee and a leg-stretch. [Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Port+Gamble+WA)
- **~14:30 — Lake Crescent:** the shoreline miles and the Lodge lawn under Mount Storm King. [Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Lake+Crescent+Lodge)
- **~16:15 — Forks Timber Museum + the Forks sign:** logging history and the Twilight photo op. [Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Forks+Timber+Museum)
