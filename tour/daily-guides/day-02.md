## Day 2 — Ruby Beach & the Long Coast (Forks, WA → Astoria, OR)

The trip's longest ride, built out of beach breaks: Ruby Beach fresh in the morning, the Kalaloch Tree of Life, coffee at the 1926 Lake Quinault Lodge, oyster country past Raymond, a Long Beach leg-stretch, and the grand 4-mile Astoria–Megler Bridge into Oregon — ending at the Cannery Pier Hotel on stilts over the Columbia. (Bowpicker's famous fish & chips is saved for tomorrow's early lunch — it keeps day-time hours.)

**Schedule:** breakfast in Forks · Ruby Beach ~09:30 · Tree of Life ~10:45 · Lake Quinault ~11:45 · Aberdeen fuel/lunch ~13:30 · Long Beach ~15:45 · the bridge ~17:00 · Cannery Pier · riverfront dinner ~18:30.

### Where to eat

**Breakfast — Forks**
- **A Shot In the Dark** (4.5★) — the town's best espresso, on the way out. [Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=A+Shot+In+the+Dark+Forks+WA)
- **Longhouse Cafe** (4.3★) — diner hotcakes and eggs, fast. [Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Longhouse+Cafe+Forks+WA)

**Lunch — Lake Quinault or Aberdeen**
- **The Salmon House** (4.4★) — cedar-plank salmon on the rainforest lake, if the day wants a long lunch. [Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Salmon+House+Restaurant+Lake+Quinault)
- **Aberdeen quick stop** — fuel both bikes, grab a sandwich, keep the schedule.

**Dinner — Astoria riverfront**
- **Buoy Beer Co.** (4.5★) — pier seafood and beer, with the glass-floor sea-lion window. [Map](https://maps.google.com/?cid=11352265591615845826)
- **South Bay Wild Fish House** (4.6★) — boat-to-table catch of the day. [Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=South+Bay+Wild+Fish+House+Astoria)
- **Fort George Brewery** (4.5★) — family brewpub; burgers for Aslan. [Map](https://maps.google.com/?cid=16478071298936810665)

### Things to do
- **~09:30 — Ruby Beach:** sea stacks and drift logs before the crowds — the day's headline. [Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Ruby+Beach+WA)
- **~10:45 — Kalaloch Tree of Life:** the root-cave spruce hanging over the void. [Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Kalaloch+Tree+of+Life)
- **~11:45 — Lake Quinault Lodge:** coffee on the lake lawn. [Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Lake+Quinault+Lodge)
- **~17:00 — Astoria–Megler Bridge & the Cannery Pier:** the 4-mile crossing, then ships past the window all evening. [Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Cannery+Pier+Hotel+Astoria)
