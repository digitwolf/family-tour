## Day 8 — The Columbia & Backroads Home (Portland → Woodinville, WA)

The big final ride, run like a tour in miniature: an early donut box, out under the St. Johns Bridge before the city wakes, US-30 up the Columbia to the Lewis & Clark Bridge, an early Castle Rock lunch, then the Cowlitz and Rainier-foothill backroads — Mount Rainier over the Orting valley — to the Woodinville driveway by dinner.

**Schedule:** donuts ~07:30 · roll ~08:15 · St. Johns Bridge ~08:45 · Lewis & Clark Bridge ~10:15 · Castle Rock lunch & fuel ~11:00 · Orting valley ~14:30 · home ~17:00.
