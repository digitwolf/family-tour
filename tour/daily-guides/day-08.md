## Day 8 — The Fast Lane Home (Portland → Woodinville, WA)

The one planned freeway day, taken by choice so the trip ends early and easy: a donut-box breakfast, an optional St. Johns Bridge photo, then I-5 north at a steady right-lane cruise — an early Castle Rock lunch at the halfway mark, Mount Rainier over the highway for the last hour, and the Woodinville driveway by mid-afternoon.
