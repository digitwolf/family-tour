## Day 3 — Haystack Rock to the Two Capes (Astoria → Tierra Del Mar, OR)

A slow Cannery Pier morning (Column gliders, an early Bowpicker lunch at open), then 100 icon-stacked coastal miles: Haystack Rock at Cannon Beach, the Neahkahnie cliff highway, the Tillamook Creamery's cheese-and-scoop marquee stop, and the little Three Capes road past Cape Meares Lighthouse and Three Arch Rocks to the geodesic domes of Two Capes Lookout — home for two nights.

**Schedule:** Astoria morning · Bowpicker ~11:00 · Haystack Rock ~12:15 · Neahkahnie ~13:30 · Creamery ~15:00 · Cape Meares ~16:15 · Oceanside ~16:45 · dome check-in ~17:30 · dinner Netarts or Pacific City.

### Where to eat

**Breakfast — Astoria**
- **Blue Scorcher Bakery & Cafe** (4.5★) — the beloved bakery morning; plain rolls keep Aslan happy. [Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Blue+Scorcher+Bakery+Astoria)

**Lunch — Astoria, early**
- **Bowpicker Fish and Chips** (4.8★) — the legendary boat-stand albacore; cash only, go at open. [Map](https://maps.google.com/?cid=10661837831491802640)
- **Backup:** hold out for the **Tillamook Creamery cafe** (grilled cheese + mac & cheese + the scoop). [Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Tillamook+Creamery)

**Dinner — Netarts / Pacific City**
- **The Schooner** (4.2★) — Netarts Bay oysters at the water's edge, 10 min from the dome. [Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=The+Schooner+Restaurant+Netarts)
- **Pelican Brewing** (4.1★) — dinner on the sand under Cape Kiwanda; kids' menu. [Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Pelican+Brewing+Pacific+City)
- **Doryland Pizza** (4.1★) — the simplest kid win in the old cannery. [Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Doryland+Pizza+Pacific+City)

### Things to do
- **~09:30 — Astoria Column:** 164 steps up, balsa glider down. [Map](https://maps.google.com/?cid=12946077969317517854)
- **~12:15 — Haystack Rock:** tidepools and puffins under the 235-ft stack. [Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Haystack+Rock+Cannon+Beach)
- **~15:00 — Tillamook Creamery:** the factory gallery, curds and famous scoops. [Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Tillamook+Creamery)
- **~16:15 — Cape Meares:** the lighthouse and Octopus Tree. [Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Cape+Meares+Lighthouse)
