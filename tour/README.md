## Destinations

A **Woodinville (Seattle) grand loop** — out around the **Olympic Peninsula**, down the **northern Oregon coast**, home through the **Willamette Valley and Portland**. **≈ 945 riding miles** over **8 days** (depart **Sat 15 August 2026**, home **Sat 22 August 2026**); average riding day ≈ 2¾h, longest day ≈ 4.3h (the Olympic-coast run). Built around **Galiya, a still-new solo rider** — so it stays **off all freeways and Interstates all week**, with one planned exception: the short **I-5 run home on Day 8**. Breaks every 60–90 minutes for **Aslan (6)**.

**Signature foodie thread:** the route strings together the coast's best bites — **Astoria fish & chips → Tillamook cheese & ice cream → Netarts Bay oysters → Yachats chowder → a Portland food-cart night** — celebrated as the **[Coast Food Trail](00-overview.md#the-coast-food-trail)** (Days 2–7).

**No flights, no rentals:** the family rides their **own** bikes round-trip from home (see [02 — Getting started](02-getting-started.md)). The only crossing is the short **Edmonds–Kingston ferry** on Day 1; the two rest days fall at the trip's two 2-night bases — the **Two Capes Lookout** glamping domes (Tue Aug 18) and oceanfront **Yachats** (Thu Aug 20).
