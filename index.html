    Riding Safely Together

    Safety & Comfort First

    This is a confidence tour — every choice is shaped around Galiya's still-new-rider pace and Aslan's comfort, not mileage.

    🛣️ New Rider (Galiya · W230)

    • Rides up front so her pace controls the group
    • No freeways or Interstates — backroads, US-101 & gentle passes
    • Short, confidence-building days; only one ~4h day
    • The ferry & frequent stops break up Day 1
    • Two rest days at the two-night bases keep the long days fresh

    🧒 Child Passenger (Aslan · GS)

    • Properly fitting helmet, jacket, gloves, pants & boots
    • Top-box backrest, grab strap & intercom
    • Must reach the pegs and sit securely
    • Stop every 60–90 minutes; never overtired
    • Snacks, rain layers & spare gloves carried by Ruslan

    ⚠️ Before You Go

    • Motorcycle endorsement on each licence (Galiya's is new)
    • Current insurance + registration on both bikes
    • Service both bikes; mind the W230's small tank & range
    • Pack for coast fog and rain and warm valley afternoons
    • Book early — August is peak season; the glamping camps sell out
